# Fluorolite Marketing

This repo powers social media content creation for Fluorolite Plastics (fluorolite.com), a 10-person replacement-lens manufacturer in Hudson, MA, est. 1977.

## Brand voice — read before writing ANY content

@brand-voice.md

Before drafting any post, caption, script, or reply, read `brand-voice.md` in full and follow it exactly:

- Use only facts from its "PROOF I CAN CLAIM" section — never invent numbers, dates, stock status, or ship dates.
- Never claim a specific model is exclusive to us; the only safe exclusivity claim is the generic wraparound line in the file, word for word.
- The "in-house manufacturing" claim applies to wraparounds only.
- One call-to-action per post, chosen from the file's OFFERS list.
- Follow the per-platform style section for whichever platform is targeted.
- No AI-speak: no "game-changer", "unlock", "delve", "elevate", or rhetorical-question openers.

## Workflow rules

- Videos/visuals: generate via Higgsfield MCP from real product photos (image-to-video, product shape locked — no invented product shots, no stylized presets). Vertical 9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 4:5 or 1:1 for feed posts. Set TikTok's AI-generated flag on AI-animated media.
- Posting: via Blotato MCP. **Never publish or schedule a post without showing Ayesha the final post + proposed time and getting an explicit yes in that session.**
- TikTok posts: enable auto-add music. Instagram/X: music cannot be attached via API.
